# Runway: video-generation assessment

**Category:** AI video generation  
**Provider:** Runway  
**Assessment status:** Not assessed  
**Account access status:** Runway documents a one-time free allocation of 125 credits; exact model availability and generation cost must be checked before testing.

## Assessment focus

Assess generated video for a defined, low-impact organisational communication scenario. Examine prompt adherence, scene continuity, temporal consistency, visual artefacts, controllability, realism, and synthetic-media risks.

## Planned test themes

1. Following a short, neutral scene description
2. Preserving requested visual details across a generated clip
3. Handling a sequence with a simple action
4. Observing continuity and temporal artefacts
5. Revising a clip after targeted feedback, if the free feature permits
6. Reviewing available controls and output limitations
7. Considering risks of misleading or deceptive synthetic video

The number of practical tests may be lower than other categories because the free credit allocation is one-time and model costs vary. These are proposed themes, not completed tests.

## Credit-preservation rule

Before generating:
1. Sign in and confirm the free credit balance.
2. Check which video model is included in the Free plan.
3. Check the credit cost for the intended clip duration and settings.
4. Record the cost before spending credits.
5. Agree on the test prompts and order.
6. Do not upgrade or purchase credits for this project without explicit approval.

## Configuration to record

- Account plan and date checked
- Model/version shown
- Free credit balance before and after each test
- Clip duration, resolution, and settings
- Watermark or output restrictions
- Any feature that requires an upgrade

## Safety and limitations

Use fictional scenes and non-identifiable subjects. Do not create impersonations or deceptive videos of real people. A small number of generated clips cannot establish general video-model reliability or safety.
