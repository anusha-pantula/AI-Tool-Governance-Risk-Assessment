## Access verification procedure

For each tool:

1. Open the official product link and sign in.
2. Confirm the exact feature required for the category is available without a paid upgrade.
3. Record the visible plan, model/version if shown, quota, watermark, and relevant restrictions.
4. Capture the observation date. A screenshot may be retained as evidence if it contains no personal or sensitive information.
5. Do not run tests until the test protocol and data-safety checks are ready.
6. If access is blocked or the product changes materially, record the limitation and discuss alternatives before substituting a tool.

## Selection and scope notes

- One tool is selected per category. This is a focused case-study sample, not a representative sample of the entire AI market.
- Selection does not imply endorsement by the project.
- Tool and model versions may change. Record the tested version and date.
- Free-plan findings must not be generalised to all paid, business, or enterprise plans.
- ChatGPT's chatbot assessment will focus on conversational use, not its image-generation features.
- Adobe Firefly's image-generation feature will be assessed in the image category.
- Runway will be assessed in the video category.
- Zapier Agents is the selected agent case study. Record product naming, interface, and any migration or transition notices observed during the assessment. Do not silently substitute another product.
- Free access is confirmed by the project owner, but exact quotas and feature-level availability remain to be documented.
