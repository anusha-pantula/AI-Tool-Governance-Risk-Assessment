## Current public access evidence

The following statements were checked against provider documentation on 11 November 2026. Plan details and product features can change.

### ChatGPT

OpenAI's Free tier documentation describes access to everyday text conversations and selected tools with separate usage limits. The available model and tool limits can change. For this project, record the model label displayed in the user's session, the plan, date, and any visible usage restrictions.

Sources:
- [ChatGPT Free Tier FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9275245-chatgpt-free-tier-faq)
- [ChatGPT pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/)

### Zapier Agents

Zapier's Free plan documentation states that free accounts can access free versions of Zapier Agents and Zapier Chatbots, alongside a monthly task allowance for the Zapier platform. A separate Agents usage page states a 400-activity monthly Free allowance and notes that Zapier is moving Agents to AI by Zapier. These are distinct usage measures described in separate product documents; do not combine them or assume that every account has the same interface. Confirm the product name, current access, and activity counter shown after sign-in.

Sources:
- [What's included in Zapier's Free plan?](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/32337438839565-What-s-included-in-Zapier-s-Free-plan)
- [How is Zapier Agents usage measured?](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/26559132765325-How-is-Zapier-Agents-usage-measured)

### Adobe Firefly

Adobe documents a free tier with limited daily generations across selected image, video, and audio models. The number of generations, models, and available features can change. Free-user allowances are not equivalent to the paid monthly credit allocations. For this project, use image-generation features only; do not treat video generation in Firefly as part of the video category assessment.

Sources:
- [Adobe Firefly plans (India)](https://www.adobe.com/in/products/firefly/plans.html)
- [Adobe generative credits FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/in/creative-cloud/apps/generative-ai/generative-credits-faq.html)

### Runway

Runway's Free plan documentation currently describes a one-time allocation of 125 credits, limited access to selected generative tools, and 5 GB asset storage. The credits do not renew. Model and feature availability can change, and free outputs may include a watermark. Check the account before designing tests.

Sources:
- [Runway Free plan details](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/50404627334547-Free-plan-details)
- [How do credits work?](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/15124877443219-How-do-credits-work)

## Access verification procedure

For each tool:

1. Open the official product link from this document.
2. Sign in or create an account using the free option. Do not enter payment details or start a paid trial for this project.
3. Navigate to the exact feature required for the category.
4. Confirm that the feature can be opened without an upgrade prompt.
5. Record the visible plan, model/version if shown, usage quota, and any watermark or restrictions.
6. Do not run tests until the test protocol and data-safety checks are ready.
7. If access is blocked, record the limitation. Do not purchase a subscription just to preserve the original shortlist; discuss a replacement first.

## Selection and scope notes

- One tool is selected per category. This is a focused case-study sample, not a representative sample of the entire AI market.
- Selection does not imply endorsement by the project.
- Tool and model versions may change during the project. Record the tested version and date.
- A free plan may have restrictions that differ from business or enterprise deployments. Findings must not be generalised to all plans.
- ChatGPT's chatbot assessment will focus on conversational use. Its image-generation feature will not be assessed in the image-generation category.
- Adobe Firefly's image-generation feature will be assessed in the image category. Its video features will not be used as the selected video case.
- Zapier Agents is the chosen agent case study, but its product transition must be documented. If the legacy product is no longer accessible, pause and obtain approval before substituting another product.
- Adobe Firefly and Runway free-generation access has been confirmed by the project owner on 12 November 2026; exact models, quotas and restrictions still need to be recorded.
- Zapier Agents account access remains unverified. ChatGPT text access is available in the current ChatGPT session; record the model label and plan details at test time.
